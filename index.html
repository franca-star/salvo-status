In the monthly brief → go straight to your tool. Not in it → Content Brief first. The Content Brief is only there to make you a brief when you don't already have one.
Don't use the Content Brief to write content — you'll just get the brief back. It makes briefs; your channel tool makes content.

Same brief, two tools

The wrong way

Brief → Content Brief → a copy of the brief

Put Rosa's brief into the Content Brief and ask for content, and it hands the brief straight back. It's a brief-maker, so that's all you get.

The right way

Brief → your channel tool → your content

Feed Rosa's brief to your tool and it picks up where you left off: 'Feed Post 2 done, Carousel 2 is next up — Italian Deli and Pragmatic Operator, Strategic lens' and offers to draft it.

What's happening this month

Monthly Brief project — live

Reverse-engineered from Rosa's signed-off Rummo brief. Deployed to the team and tested on the August Sammontana brief with a strong result. The operational frame that keeps every channel aligned to the month's priorities.

Personas v1.1 — signed off

Signed off by Rosa and Jay. Four buyer personas (3 existing + the new Italian Deli / Cafe), with real customer examples, customer voice from reviews, buyer-lens overlay and commercial focus per persona. A set of visual reference cards is now shared with the team.

Content Brief & Instagram — on personas v1.1

Both updated to the signed-off v1.1 personas and live for the team, so every brief and every post threads the four personas and Jay's commercial framing.

Interim Email tool for Vicky + a team guide

A stopgap Email project, now built and in testing with Vicky, so she has brand-aware support ahead of the full email tool (which waits for HubSpot). It learns from Lisa's craft standard and Salvo's best-performing past emails. Alongside it, a short 'how the system works' guide so the team uses the tools the right way.

House of Pasta — Campaign Brief project

The first proper Campaign Brief project, built around the House of Pasta launch — a separate division with its own landing page, brand identity and social handle. Tasting 13 July, launch end September. We build the brief from Rosa's strategy once it's ready.

What's coming next

LinkedIn and WhatsApp tools built once the brief structure is stable, so they ladder up to the new framework cleanly. The full email tool waits until Salvo is on HubSpot (not before).
