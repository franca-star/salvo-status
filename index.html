How the tools work, and where the build is up to. Built with Lionstar Social.

Report an issue or give feedback

Every piece of work starts with a brief. The brief is where the thinking happens once: who it's for, what we're saying, what we want to happen, what's in it, when. Write it once and everyone works from the same page. Then a writing tool turns it into copy for your channel. The tools already hold the brand, so you bring the brief and keep the prompt simple.

Two kinds of tool

Planning tools

Turn intent into a brief

Monthly Brief (the month's activity, Rosa) · Campaign Brief (an integrated campaign across every channel, Rosa; House of Pasta was the first) · Content Brief (a quick brief for a one-off that isn't in either, usually Liv)

Writing tools

Turn a brief into copy for one channel

Instagram (Liv) · LinkedIn (Liv) · WhatsApp (Egle) · Email (Vicky, with Lisa). Each one holds the brand, the personas and the rules for its channel, and asks you for what it's missing.

Been given a brief? Go straight to your writing tool. No brief for this piece? Make a quick one in the Content Brief tool first. The Content Brief tool makes briefs. It doesn't write posts, mailers or broadcasts.
Don't ask the Content Brief tool for content. It's a planning tool, so it hands you a brief. Your writing tool makes the content.

Same brief, two tools

The wrong way

Rosa's brief → Content Brief tool → the same brief back

Paste a finished brief into a planning tool and ask for a post, and you get the brief handed back to you, reworded. That's what happened with the Rummo brief in July. A planning tool can only make briefs.

The right way

Rosa's brief → your writing tool → your content

Paste the same brief into the Instagram tool and it picks up where the brief left off, names the next post in the plan ('Feed Post 2 done, Carousel 2 is next, for delis and multi-site owners') and offers to draft it.
